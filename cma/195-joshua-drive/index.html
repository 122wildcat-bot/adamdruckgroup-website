    Methodology, data notes and sources

    How the adjustments work

    • Above-grade living area: $90 per square foot of difference.
    • Finished lower level: $35 per square foot of difference, with no extra bedroom premium.
    • Full bath difference: about $10,000 per full bath. Your home’s 3.5 baths include a full bath on the finished lower level. Pool, recent rebuilds and larger lots get individual adjustments, shown on each sale.
    • Slower market: the adjusted figure is reduced 3% once. This is a pricing cushion, not a measured drop in neighborhood values.
