About Katie

Rooted in Central Pennsylvania

Born and raised in Central Pennsylvania, Katie knows York and the surrounding communities inside and out. Those roots give her a grounded perspective on the local market, and her care for the people she works with makes the process feel less overwhelming.

Katie focuses on what matters most to you, whether that's buying your first home, selling a property, or exploring a commercial opportunity. She takes the time to listen, explain every step, and make sure you feel confident in each decision.

Clients get a partner who combines local knowledge, careful negotiation, and steady communication from first conversation to closing.
